# Moon surface

`moon-map.jpg`: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit, LROC WAC color mosaic. Credit: NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright, NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, LRO/LROC.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Asset: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg

The app projects this map onto a sphere and shades it using the daily SunCalc lunar phase. Its decorative orientation does not model libration or exact observer tilt.

`moon-map-illustrated.png`: AI-generated watercolor/ink interpretation of the NASA map above, with `home-portrait.png` as a style reference. Retains recognizable lunar features; artistic interpretation is not a scientific map. Astronomical phase and daily shading are calculated separately.

`moon-map-minimal.png`: AI-generated minimalist acrylic-and-ink interpretation of the NASA lunar map, guided by the user’s editorial paper-cover illustration prompt. Four main color families, simplified maria, sparse crater marks, and subtle handmade paper texture. Artistic surface details; astronomical phase remains calculated separately.
